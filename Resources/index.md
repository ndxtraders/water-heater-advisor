# Water Heater Resources for Homeowners

A water-heater decision gets expensive when the wrong question comes first.

Start with what is happening now. A leak needs a different answer than a system that runs out of hot water. A failed twelve-year-old tank creates a different decision than a working heater in a planned remodel. And a national price range cannot tell you what gas-line, venting, electrical, permit, or local utility work your home needs.

This library is organized around those decisions.

You will get the general answer first. When the right answer depends on your household, your home, or your local market, we will show you exactly which details change it.

## Do you have an urgent problem?

Water on the floor, no hot water, signs of overheating, or a concern involving gas or combustion should not begin with a shopping guide.

Use the [water-heater emergency guide](/emergency) for immediate safety steps and the point where you need an emergency service or licensed professional.

If the unit is still working but something has changed, start here:

- [How do I know my water heater is going bad?](/resources/water-heater-failure-warning-signs)
- [Why am I running out of hot water?](/resources/running-out-of-hot-water)

## Should you repair it or replace it?

Age matters. It just does not decide the answer by itself.

A useful repair-or-replace decision also considers where the failure occurred, whether the tank is sound, what the warranty covers, whether parts are available, what damage a leak could cause, and whether the current system still fits the home.

- [Should I repair or replace my water heater?](/resources/repair-or-replace-water-heater)
- [How long do water heaters last?](/resources/how-long-water-heaters-last)
- [What are the warning signs of water-heater failure?](/resources/water-heater-failure-warning-signs)

## What type and size fit your home?

The gallon number on the box is only part of a storage water heater’s capacity. First-hour rating and recovery matter too. Tankless sizing uses another set of facts: simultaneous flow and the temperature rise between incoming water and the hot-water setting.

Start with the full sizing guide, then use the narrower explainers if one rating is holding up the decision.
